# ORBIT, sales video script (2 min)

Very short sentences, one idea each. **H** = Himalaya, **M** = Marceau. *[TEXT]* = big white title on black.

## 0:00 to 0:15, the hook
*Black screen. Hackathon crowd noise.*
*[TEXT] Tonight. Tsai CITY.*
*Quick shot: the two of you at your laptops, the room full.*

**H:** Two hours ago, we didn't know this hackathon existed.
**M:** We found it by luck.
**H:** And that's the problem.

## 0:15 to 0:35, the pain
*Fast scroll through Yale Connect. Then an inbox: mailing lists, again and again.*
*[TEXT] 400+ events this week.*

**M:** Yale posts more than four hundred events every week.
**H:** On sites nobody opens. In emails nobody reads.
**M:** So we miss the talk that could change our life.
**H:** And the people we were supposed to meet there.
*[TEXT] You will hear about almost none.*

## 0:35 to 0:50, the reveal
*One second of black and silence. Then Orbit's onboarding: "Continue with Yale NetID", the sources checking in one by one.*

**M:** So we built Orbit.
**H:** You log in with your NetID. That's it.
**M:** Orbit reads all of Yale for you. Your classes. Every calendar. Every club. Every mailing list.
**H:** Then it asks you one question.
*[ON SCREEN] What are you building toward?*

## 0:50 to 1:25, the demo
*Type "Break into the space industry". The 3D night map, six gold beams rising one by one.*

**H:** I want to work in space.
**M:** Grok reads every event of the week, checks her schedule, and lights up the six that get her there.
*Click the first card: Why you, It fits, friends going.*
**H:** It tells me why it's for me. When to leave. Who's already going.
*The wow moment: the camera reaches the "You are here" pin, the Grokathon, with Marceau's avatar on it. Slow down here.*
**M:** And look. Tonight's hackathon.
**H:** Orbit would have told us.
*Switch the goal to "Meet people outside my major". The map lights up somewhere else.*
**M:** New goal. New campus. Real Yale events.

## 1:25 to 1:42, the human heart
*Click Tonight?, then Stressed. Three light-blue beams.*

**H:** And on the hard nights, Orbit doesn't just ask what you want.
**M:** It asks how you feel.
**H:** Three things nearby. Each one backed by research. A nudge, not therapy.

## 1:42 to 2:00, the close
*Back to the two of you in the room. Then the map turning slowly with its gold beams.*

**M:** Tonight, hundreds of brilliant students missed this room.
**H:** Next week, none of them will.
*[TEXT] ORBIT*
*[TEXT] Find the room. And the people to walk in with.*

---

## Why it works

- **The same story opens and closes the video**: "we found it by luck", then "none of them will". The jury remembers the loop.
- **One number**, 400+, and it is true (212 events on events.yale.edu + 213 on Yale Connect for the next 8 days, counted on 17 November 2026).
- **One wow moment**: the hackathon pin with Marceau on it. The app tells your own story.
- **Silence matters**: one second of black before "So we built Orbit".

## How to shoot it

1. Film the two shots of you in the room with a phone, 5 seconds each.
2. Screen-record the app following the exact click order above (reload the page to get the onboarding; add `?skip=1` to skip it).
3. Record the voices separately, somewhere quiet, then edit the screen recording on top.
4. Music that builds slowly and cuts dead on the black screen before "So we built Orbit".

## The app's onboarding (same story)

1. **The problem**: "Yale posts 400+ events this week. You will hear about almost none." One button: "Continue with Yale NetID". Under it: "No install. No sign-up. Every student is already on it."
2. **Reading your Yale**: Canvas, Yale calendar, Yale Connect, Mailing lists, Friends check in one by one with their real counts, then "Orbit knows your Yale." and the button "Show me my week".
3. **The question** on the map: "What are you building toward?"

Code: `components/ui/Onboarding.tsx`.

## Honesty lines (if a judge asks)

- "Real Yale calendar and Yale Connect events, plus sample ones for the demo." The screen already shows "Preview · sample data".
- Mailing lists: "Next, Orbit reads the mailing lists too." (the three list events in the demo are samples).
- The science: "Each pick is matched to a type of activity that research links to that feeling. A nudge, not therapy."
